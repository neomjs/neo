# A lease-caused yield cannot release the lease, because the sweep never learns which cause stopped it

## Context

Split out of #17398 after @neo-gpt-emmy's Drop+Supersede of PR #17399 ([review](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/17399#pullrequestreview-4981756492)). Her corrected prescription names two obligations, and they are not one PR: **own both outer-acquisition paths** (#17398, amended) and **a lease-specific sweep-exit contract** (this leaf).

The starvation being fixed is live and dated. On an external tenant deployment, 2026-08-20: five maintenance tasks deferred since Aug 18–19 under holder `tenant-repo-sync` — `graphlog-compaction`, `memory-summary-backfill`, `message-concept-harvest`, `provider-residency-repair`, `summary` — with the orchestrator logging *"the fairness yield bound has been exceeded; the lease pipeline is not admitting its waiters."*

## The Problem

Two yield causes require **different** exits, and the vote that reports them collapses to a boolean.

| yield cause | required exit |
|---|---|
| slice budget expired | rotate to the next repo; the lease is still ours, keep it |
| outer lease outlived its hold bound | stop admitting the tail after the active cohort, commit resumable state, **release the outer lease** |

The refuted implementation composed voters with `voters.some(voter => voter() === true)`. That is a shape error rather than a detail: **all three lease-exit steps are conditional on the cause the OR discards.** A sweep that learns only *"someone said stop"* cannot decide whether to keep rotating or to release, so it keeps rotating — which is precisely the observed behaviour, a holder that never admits its waiters.

Falsifier coordinates from the review: per-repo vote `TenantRepoSyncService.mjs:2401-2417`; all-repo admission `TenantRepoSyncService.mjs:2922-2923`; watchdog owner projection `pipeline.mjs:786-787`.

## The Architectural Reality

The vote and the exit are separately owned. #17398 owns producing a cause-bearing verdict from both outer acquisitions; this leaf owns **consuming** it. The dependency is one-directional and strict: until a yield carries its cause, no exit branch here is expressible, so #17398 lands first.

ADR 0022 governs the mechanism: cooperative yield only. Releasing the outer lease on a lease-caused yield is the cooperative act — it is the holder standing down, not a preemption of it. #17379 died proposing hard preemption and that is not revisited here.

## Contract Ledger Matrix

| Target Surface | Source of Authority | Proposed Behavior | Fallback | Docs | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sweep admission, `TenantRepoSyncService.mjs:2922-2923` | the yield verdict's cause (from #17398) | a lease cause stops admitting further repos after the active cohort | no cause / slice cause ⇒ rotation continues, unchanged | cloud-deployment docs | 5 waiters starved past the bound under one holder |
| active-cohort checkpoint | the per-repo resumable state already written on partial progress | committed before the lease is released, so the next acquisition resumes rather than re-materializes | commit failure ⇒ hold the lease and report, never release over lost progress | — | a released lease over uncommitted state re-buys the corpus |
| outer lease release | the acquisition that produced the cause | released after the cohort settles and state is committed | a cause the sweep cannot attribute to an acquisition ⇒ hold and report loudly | — | the holder currently never releases |
| slice-cause rotation | `sliceBudgetMs` | unchanged, and asserted as an independent control | — | — | the lease exit must not absorb slice behaviour |

## Decision Record impact

`aligned-with ADR 0022` — cooperative yield only; no hard preemption, no `leaseMonitor.mjs` wiring.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **RED-PROOF, lease cause:** a yield carrying a lease cause stops tail admission after the active cohort, commits that cohort's resumable state, and releases the outer lease. Asserted on the production admission path with the release observed, not on a stub that records the intent.
- [ ] **SILENT ARM, slice cause:** a yield carrying a slice cause rotates to the next repo and does **not** release the lease. Without this arm, an implementation that releases on every yield passes the arm above while destroying slice behaviour.
- [ ] **Ordering is a contract, not an accident.** The lease is released only after the cohort's state is committed. Asserted by a commit failure: the lease is **held** and the condition reported, never released over lost progress.
- [ ] **An unattributable cause holds rather than releases.** A yield whose cause cannot be tied to an acquisition fails loud and keeps the lease — a breaker that releases on ambiguity is worse than one that holds.
- [ ] **Every production propagation edge is mutation-guarded.** Deleting any one of the three lease-exit steps turns exactly one arm red. Stub-only dispatch evidence does not close this: the prior implementation's tests traversed a stub rather than the production edges, and that is why a green suite shipped a shape that could not release.
- [ ] **Plane-observable outcome:** the watchdog's starved-waiter list drains under holder `tenant-repo-sync` rather than growing. Recorded as a post-merge observation with its own query, not as a merge gate — the merge gate is the arms above.

## Out of Scope

- Producing the cause. #17398 owns both outer-acquisition paths and the cause-bearing verdict; this leaf consumes it and lands second.
- Hard preemption of a live holder — ADR 0022 anti-anchors it and #17379 died proposing it.
- The slice budget's own value or phase accounting. Separate concern, and the epic #17411 lane covers what consumes the budget.

## Avoided Traps

- **Do not re-introduce a boolean vote.** A verdict that answers only true/false makes every requirement on this ticket unexpressible. That was the refuted PR's central error, and it was written as the elegant part.
- **Do not release the lease before committing state.** A released lease over uncommitted progress re-buys the corpus on the next acquisition, which on a slow lane is the difference between a corpus that grows and one that burns compute at a constant count.
- **Do not assume one outer holder.** The prior ticket named the CLI; `MaintenanceBackpressureService.mjs:992-998` is a second outer acquisition, indistinguishable from it at the vote. That premise error is corrected on #17398 and must not be re-imported here.
- **Do not prove the exit with a stub.** A stub records that the code intended to release. The starvation is evidence that intent is not the failing step.
