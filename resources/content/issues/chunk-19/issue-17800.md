# Correct ADR 0040 learn/agentos custody language for the cut manifest

## Context

[#17787](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/17787)'s **AC-4 pins "the merged ADR 0040 correction"** as a prerequisite coordinate. The cut manifest cannot bind without it, and three Wave-3 leaves (#17788, #17789, #17791) cannot proceed without the manifest. This is the head of the split's critical path, and until now no open issue owned it — the gap was found by @neo-gpt-emmy while V-B-A'ing [D#17782](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/17782)'s lane table against executable authority.

Live latest-open sweep: checked the latest 20 open issues at 2026-08-26T12:20:00Z; no equivalent found. A2A in-flight claim sweep over 30 messages (all read-states): no competing `[lane-claim]` on this scope.

## The Problem

ADR 0040 §2.7 reads:

> Staying in `neomjs/neo` in wave one: `apps/**` (the FM's later home is its own decision), published `learn/agentos` content and every Portal/SEO/tree input derived from it, `resources/content` mirrors, `src/ai/**`, and the minimal Engine contributor surface.

**The ADRs themselves live at `learn/agentos/decisions/**`** — inside the very directory that sentence keeps Engine. So "published `learn/agentos` content" is ambiguous about whether Agent OS decision records and Brain learning guides are Engine-staying published content or Brain substrate. The cut manifest must classify `learn/agentos/**` rows, and it cannot while one sentence answers both ways.

Two authorities now bear on it and they disagree with §2.7 as written:

1. **Fresh operator direction (2026-08-26, this turn):** Brain learning guides, the ADR split, and Brain tests move to the Brain — and that affects CI workflows.
2. **ADR 0040 §5 fires on exactly this:** its *Revalidation triggers* list "custody changes to `apps/**`, **`learn/agentos`**, or `src/ai/**`" as reopening the record. A `learn/agentos` custody change is not an edit around the ADR — it is a trigger the ADR itself declares.

`#17500` still carries the older all-`learn`-stays boundary (line 52 keeps `learn/agentos` Engine in wave one; line 132 lists moving it as Out of Scope). That boundary is superseded by (1) and (2), and this ticket records the supersession rather than quietly diverging from it.

## The Architectural Reality

- `learn/agentos/decisions/0040-agentos-extraction-topology.md` §2.7 — the ambiguous custody sentence; §5 — the revalidation trigger that fires.
- `#17787` AC-4 — pins the **merged** correction; a draft or an open PR does not satisfy it.
- `#17783` AC-7 also amends §2.7, scoped to **enforcement** custody. **Two tickets therefore edit one section** — whichever merges second rebases rather than clobbers. Flagged here so the collision is known, not discovered.
- @neo-opus-grace withdrew her §2.7 skill-custody claim as overreach earlier today, so §2.7 is independently known to over-claim; this correction and that withdrawal touch the same section.

## The Fix

Amend ADR 0040 so `learn/agentos` custody is unambiguous for manifest classification:

1. **§2.7** — replace "published `learn/agentos` content" with a **subject-keyed** split, applying §2.7's own "custody follows the SUBJECT, not the directory" rule to `learn/agentos` rather than restating it: guides and decision records whose **subject is the Agent OS** are extracted-repository custody by exact-identity census; guides and decision records whose **subject is the Body or Engine** stay in `neomjs/neo` despite sharing the directory; rendered Portal/SEO/tree inputs and `resources/content` mirrors stay Engine because their consumer is the published site.

   **`decisions/` is no exception, and this is the whole point** *(corrected 2026-08-26 after @neo-gpt-emmy's review of PR #17801)*: an earlier draft of this ticket made the 40-record decisions directory Brain custody wholesale, which reproduces the location heuristic one level lower. `0029-docking-design.md` (multi-window layout, cross-window drag) is filed beside `0027-autonomous-data-recovery-actuator.md` (Memory Core); `DockZoneModel.md` sits beside `A2A.md`. A blanket directory rule relocates the Body's design records into the Brain.

   **§2.7 names the categories and the invariant; it does not enumerate.** Exact per-file identities are #17787's cut-manifest census — that is where a mixed corpus gets resolved file by file with evidence.
2. **§5** — record that this revalidation trigger fired and is discharged by this amendment, naming the operator direction as its authority.

Content edit only. **No file moves in this ticket.**

## Decision Record impact

`amends ADR 0040` — §2.7 custody boundary and §5 trigger discharge. No new topology is invented; the amendment applies §2.7's existing subject rule to a directory the sentence had treated by location.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-1** — §2.7 no longer uses "published `learn/agentos` content" as one undivided category, and does not replace it with a directory rule. Custody is keyed by **subject** at every level: Agent-OS-subject guides and decision records are extracted-repository custody by exact-identity census; Body/Engine-subject guides and decision records stay in `neomjs/neo` **despite sharing the same directories**; rendered Portal/SEO/tree inputs and `resources/content` mirrors stay Engine. The section must carry a **negative specimen** proving `decisions/` is not a blanket — a Body-subject record filed beside an Agent-OS one.
- [ ] **AC-2** — §5 records the `learn/agentos` revalidation trigger as fired and discharged, citing the operator direction that fired it.
- [ ] **AC-3** — a reader can determine from §2.7 alone **which category** any `learn/agentos/**` path falls into and that **subject, not location, is the classifier**. §2.7 does **not** owe the per-file enumeration — exact identities are #17787's census, and claiming otherwise would put prose where a file-by-file evidenced census belongs. Demonstrated in the PR against **four real tracked paths — a positive and a negative at both the guide and the decision-record level**, no placeholders.
- [ ] **AC-4** — `#17500`'s superseded all-`learn`-stays boundary is reconciled: either its body is truth-synced or this amendment explicitly names it as superseded, so the two do not stand in contradiction.
- [ ] **AC-5** — merged, not merely open. `#17787` AC-4 pins the **merged** correction; this ticket is not resolved by an approved PR.

## Out of Scope

- **The migration itself.** Moving Brain guides and ADRs into `neomjs/neo-agent-brain` is a **separate one-PR Brain-receive leaf** (successor, still to be filed); `#17791` owns the eventual Neo-side removal. This ticket makes the boundary decidable; it moves nothing.
- Brain test migration and the CI-workflow consequences the operator named alongside the ADR split — same successor family, not this leaf.
- §2.7's enforcement-custody clause — `#17783` AC-7.
- Portal/SEO/tree input custody — stays Engine, unchanged.

## Avoided Traps

- **Fusing correction with migration.** One leaf doing both would have blocked `#17787`'s fast unblock behind a cross-repo multi-PR move, and would have inflated a content edit into a custody transfer. Two repo-owned leaves are simpler than one cross-repo ticket (@neo-gpt-emmy's convergence).
- **Diverging from `#17500` silently.** Its boundary genuinely conflicts; AC-4 forces reconciliation instead of leaving two contradictory authorities standing.
- **Editing §2.7 blind to `#17783`.** Two tickets amend one section; naming it here beats a merge-time surprise.
