# Right-size the vector-generation election substrate after the cutover proves what earned its existence

The same standard the merge gate applied to PR #17031 (2,753-line runner → #17036), applied to my own artifact: the election substrate on PR #17029 landed at ~1,180 lines in `generationElectionStore.mjs` plus the gated-promote helper and a 540-line spec, and its quiesce/fence/reconcile apparatus grew under two review cycles that each ADDED defensive machinery. Some of it is load-bearing; some of it may be armor that a one-cutover reality never exercises. This ticket owns finding out WITH EVIDENCE and reducing accordingly — it must not be forgotten, and it must not run before the evidence exists.

Live latest-open sweep: checked latest open issues at 2026-08-12T19:58Z; #17036 covers the runner split and carries the generator fix (LOC tripwire OR review-template file-shape row); no ticket covers the election substrate itself.

## Context

Today's merge-gate calibration named the class: review culture optimizing for DEFENSIBILITY grows code — every falsifier answered adds a mechanism — while nobody holds the mandate to say "this should be a third the size." The election substrate is the second-largest artifact of the wave and I authored it, so it gets the same post-evidence right-sizing #17036 prescribes for the runner.

## The Problem

Candidate over-machinery, to be judged against the RECEIPTS after the canonical run + cutover (#17024/#17025/#17026):

- The quiesce window contract (declare/renew/dual-check) vs the reality of ONE operator-controlled cutover window — if the cutover executes under a single quiesce with no renewal and no mid-window expiry, the renewal op and the second-rename re-check may be armor without a threat model.
- The `rolled-back` arm's full un-park gating vs whether rollback is ever exercised outside fixtures.
- Legacy-mode grandfathering vs a plane fleet that will ALL have baselines after the first cutover — the legacy branch may be removable debt within weeks.
- The reconcile's four dispositions vs which interrupted states can physically occur under the runner's actual execution order.

## The Architectural Reality

Reduction candidates live in `ai/services/shared/vector/generationElectionStore.mjs`, `electionGatedPromote.mjs`, and their specs; consumers are the two service seams, the #17025 runner, and the #17026 manifest generator. Any reduction is a consumer-visible contract change and must sweep those five call sites.

## The Fix

1. After the canonical run, cutover, and acceptance receipts exist: audit every mechanism against whether a receipt exercised it (fixture-only exercise does not count as production need — it counts as the fixture following the mechanism into existence).
2. Remove or collapse what nothing exercised, with the removal PR citing the receipts; keep what the receipts touched.
3. Fold the retained surface into the #17036 generator decision (the LOC tripwire or review-template row applies here identically).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A mechanism-vs-receipt audit table exists on this ticket, one row per exported operation and guard, citing the receipt (or absence) that judged it.
- [ ] Everything unexercised by production receipts is removed or collapsed in one PR with golden-behavior equivalence for the retained surface (all consumer suites green unmodified except imports).
- [ ] The sequencing gate held: no reduction lands before the #17026 acceptance receipts exist.
