03

Two invariants the map forces

Ledger discipline, not decoration

The honest-null rule is visible in the render

WINDOW_SCOPED_VELOCITY_FIELDS are null — not 0, not a repeated window count — on per-agent partitions. So the five headline blocks render on the unified track; only the activity strip is per-agent. A per-agent "merged: 0" would assert a measurement never taken — and seed the silent double-count the moment a consumer aggregates. Killed at the design bar.

ADR-0032 §2.2

Every rendered claim shows its provenance

Each block carries sourceAuthority + observedAt (visible or summoned); a fact past its cadence renders stale-as-stale (the amber chip), and direction-shift renders advisory (notAuthority) — never a certainty verdict. The digest reports what the pyramid measured, at the confidence the pyramid held.
