# Neo tickets required before an external plane's next deployment can be stable

## What this is

**The Neo tickets that must be resolved before an external plane's next deployment, for it to finally be stable.** Nothing else. Ownership stays where it is — this re-parents nothing, and each owner's ticket body is authoritative over the one-line summaries here, which will go stale.

Grounded in a live measurement of that plane on 2026-08-08: its Knowledge Base has **never ingested** (`count: 0`), all four tenant repos sit `backoff-suppressed` at `consecutiveFailures: 13` with cadence pinned to the 2 h cap, `accessReadiness` is **ready 4/4** (so not auth, not access), and a single embedding of a **three-word** string did not complete in **120 s**.

**Read this first, or the list misleads:** the *cause* of the empty corpus is provider capacity on their side — one ollama serving a 26B chat model at ~399 % CPU, with the embedder contending against it. That is theirs, not ours, and **no ticket below makes a starved provider fast.** What the tickets below do is make the failure survivable, visible, and recoverable — which is the difference between "it broke again" and "it broke, we saw it, and it came back on its own." That is what *stable* has to mean here.
