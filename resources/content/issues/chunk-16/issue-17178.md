# Round 2 becomes a disposition over Round 1, with its own validation tier

Refs #17141
Refs #17163

The substrate half of #17141, and the sibling of #17163 — which carried the runtime half (per-family review budget + repair-minted re-entry, ACs 1-2) and merged as PR #17164. This leaf owns **ACs 5, 6 and 7**: the review-asset shape and its byte budget. #17141 stays open for ACs 3, 4, 8, 9 and 10.

## Problem

Round 2 of a PR review is currently a smaller copy of Round 1. It carries the same fourteen sections, the same anchors, and the same permission to mint a fresh list of Required Actions — so a second round can quietly restate, renumber, or expand the first round's demands instead of answering them. The author then negotiates against a moving target, and the thread grows without converging.

That is the mechanism #17141 exists to close: **Round 2 should be a disposition over Round 1, not a re-review.** Each Round-1 required action gets quoted verbatim and marked `ADDRESSED` / `DEFENDED` / `STILL_OPEN`. A `STILL_OPEN` item keeps the original review authoritative rather than generating a replacement packet.

## The Architectural Reality

- The canonical validator requires four premise anchors on every review body. A disposition round routed through it is **refused for omitting exactly what the new shape removes** — the guard enforces the structure the substrate is replacing, so the asset cannot land without a validation tier of its own.
- That tier has precedent in the same file: `micro-review` and `micro-delta` already establish minimal floors beside the canonical one. This is a third entry in an existing pattern, not a new mechanism.
- **`Premise Coherence` must not be relaxed globally to make room.** That anchor exists because a green checklist over a wrong premise is theater; weakening it for every shape to fit one new shape is the cheap fix and the wrong one.
- The review-body contract is **duplicated** between `ai/services/github-workflow/PullRequestService.mjs` and `.github/workflows/agent-pr-review-body-lint.yml`. Each carries its own copy of the shape constants, so a new format must land in both or the managed path and CI disagree about what is valid. That duplication is unowned and predates this work.
- Two downstream docs teach the superseded contract: `CodebaseOverview.md` describes "two ordinary RCs, then `COMMENTED` closure", and `ProgressiveDisclosureSkills.md` describes the same two-round shape. A doc that outlives the rule it describes is how the old model gets re-derived by the next reader.

## The Fix

1. A disposition-only Round-2 asset: anchor, the verbatim carried-action table with its three dispositions, and a verdict. Nothing that mints new actions.
2. A Round-2 validation tier beside `micro-review` and `micro-delta`, dispatched before the follow-up tier, leaving `Premise Coherence` intact for every existing shape.
3. Registration in **both** contract copies — service and CI workflow.
4. Round-1 guidance tightened to require artifact/population execution or a carried executable falsifier (AC-6).
5. Both downstream docs updated to the shipped contract.

## Out of Scope

- ACs 3, 4, 8, 9, 10 of #17141 — the COMMENT-channel refusal, direct-bypass telemetry, the plain-APPROVE default, the #17103 cohort receipt, and the full falsifier matrix. Those stay on the parent.
- Deduplicating the service/CI contract copies. Named here because this leaf pays the cost of it; fixing it is a separate owner.
- The exceptional Drop+Supersede template, which deliberately keeps full structure — AC-5 scopes the reduction to *ordinary* Round 2 only.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Cycle 2's asset is disposition-only over verbatim prior actions. `STILL_OPEN` preserves the original `CHANGES_REQUESTED` without creating a new action packet; ordinary fresh findings become accepted risk. *(=#17141 AC-5)*
- [ ] Round-1 guidance requires artifact/population execution or a carried executable falsifier, including citation-inversion, full-suite, and claimed-domain boundary checks derived from the measured escape ledger. *(=#17141 AC-6)*
- [ ] The always-loaded `pr-review/SKILL.md` router has zero positive byte delta; the PR body includes the `/turn-memory-pre-flight` Map-vs-Atlas audit. *(=#17141 AC-7)*
- [ ] Total `.agents/skills/pr-review/**` bytes and procedural steps net-decrease **or** the growth is discharged through `lint-skill-manifest`'s sanctioned `[skill-growth-justified: …]` marker with a stated retirement trigger — the ordinary-Round-2 step count must fall regardless.
- [ ] A disposition body validates through `validate_pr_review_body` at the new tier rather than the canonical one, and the canonical tier still refuses a body missing `Premise Coherence`.
- [ ] Both contract copies — service and CI workflow — recognise the new shape, so the managed path and CI agree.
- [ ] `CodebaseOverview.md` and `ProgressiveDisclosureSkills.md` describe the shipped contract, with no surviving reference to the superseded two-round shape.
