# Workstation tear-out: vessel shell, DockTearOut composition, journey executor

## Context

Increment 1 of the `#15252` capstone ladder — filed as its own close-target per the operator rule `#12367` (one `Resolves` per ticket; a ticket needing N PRs becomes subs or is split). The 2026-07-25 reshape replaced the one-PR-vehicle plan with an increment ladder: this ticket (tear-out, **delivered by PR #15840**), then `#15906` (cross-window docking), then `#15252` itself (the film, the capstone's close condition).

## The Problem

`apps/workstation` had ZERO multi-window wiring: no vessel shell, no tear-out composition, no gesture executors — the flagship could not demonstrate the engine's window-independent live-state docking. The five-beat journey's scenes 2 (tear-out) and the morph (out-and-back in one drag, the film's cold-open) had no runnable substrate.

## The Architectural Reality

Thin-consumer composition of shipped `src/dashboard` machinery (epic `#15239` trap list honored — no new mechanism): `createDockTearOutHandlers` (admission chain + commit-at-terminal), `createDockVesselEmbodiment` (live-pane staging with exact-slot placeholder), `DockLayoutAdapter`'s `enableDockTearOut` opt-in (window-boundary hysteresis + overdrag), the dockdemo `?popout=` vessel-shell sibling pattern, and the `WindowPosition` main-thread addon. Two engine defects extracted en route ship separately (both merged): `#15895` (re-entry ratio normalization) and `#15899` (entry fire on placeholder-less zones) — this increment's morph witness was their discovery instrument and remains their consumer-grain regression proof.

## The Fix (delivered by PR #15840)

Viewport `?popout=` empty-vessel-host boot mode · Workspace tear-out composition over workstation seams (vessel owner grants with native-route validation, admission-token connect flow, exact-position reintegration, `preserveItemIds` projection support, worker connect/disconnect lifecycle) · `executeTearOutStep` real-pointer executor with film tunables and three terminals (commit / cancel / re-enter) plus permanent three-hop diagnostic probes · `NEO_FILM_TAKE=1` capture mode.
