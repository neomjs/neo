4. **Family rail sub-perceptible at 720p** — 3px → 4px (both the component rule and the card's nested absolute rule + the card's left pad). Film-legible without changing the token vocabulary.

Also hardened `.fm-detail-sources-axis` to block layout — the three-source readout reads as three lines even where the flex container's styling is lost.
