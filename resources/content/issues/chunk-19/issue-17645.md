# Promote exact launch-root and dependency authority into the AgentOS inventory

## Context

Epic #17500 requires #17533's paired plane-boundary receipt before any relocation leaf starts. The parent proof has already delivered its isolated two-root resolution layer, and #17634 / PR #17641 delivered H2: exact runtime-probe eligibility. Vega's current parent handover names H1 as the remaining open help-shape: promote launch-root identities and dependency populations into first-class inventory authority before the static/runtime composer consumes them.

Fresh V-B-A at origin/dev f47f337809 found:

- readEntrypoints() derives 74 unique launch roots: 62 npm, 5 workflow, 7 task; 47 resolve to Edge custody, but H2 currently governs 45 because its script-module join omits the two Edge task roots outside ai/scripts (ai/daemons/wake/daemon.mjs and ai/mcp/server/neural-link/run-bridge.mjs);
- agentOsExtractionInventory v2 retains only launchRoots.total and byVia counts, so an identity can disappear or be same-count substituted without the receipt exposing the row;
- package.json declares 38 devDependencies and zero dependencies; package.brain.json declares 3 devDependencies and zero dependencies;
- none of those 41 declaration identities is a reconciled inventory surface;
- #17533's live resolution-layer receipt therefore uses root devDependencies minus the Brain tier as an explicitly interim population and routes that topology finding to H1.

## The Problem

The paired proof cannot truthfully materialize its Edge root and nested Cloud fixture from the extraction receipt while the receipt drops the exact manifest inputs. A count-only launch population can stay green after one target is replaced by another. A dependency population inferred ad hoc by the consumer can omit a Cloud driver, absorb an Engine-only build dependency into Edge, or drift independently from the inventory registry.

That would make the proof strongest around its resolver mechanics and weakest at the population boundary that licenses every resolver result.

## The Architectural Reality

- ai/scripts/lint/lint-script-plane.mjs owns the unique launch-root population across npm, workflow, and task channels. This lane consumes readEntrypoints(); it does not create a second classifier.
- collectScriptModules() already carries the exact launch-root objects internally and discards them only when buildInventory() reduces them to counts.
- package.json and package.brain.json are the declaration sources. The inventory records declared section/name/version identity plus explicit manifestTargets (`engine`, `edge`, `cloud`, or the ADR-admitted physical `shared` package); source-declaration custody remains one disjoint disposition authority.
- reconcileInventory() and agentOsExtractionInventory.json already own exact bidirectional disposition authority, typed residue, deterministic rows, and mutation controls. New populations extend that substrate.
- ADR 0040 keeps the future repository root Host Edge, cloud/ independently installed, and forbids workspaces. H1 supplies proof inputs; it does not author the final manifests or move source.

## The Fix

1. Add an exact launch-root inventory surface derived from readEntrypoints(). Each row has a stable target identity plus the source channel/name evidence retained by the owning classifier.
2. Add an exact package-dependency inventory surface derived from package.json and package.brain.json. Identity binds manifest, dependency section, and package name; evidence carries the declared version.
3. Reconcile both surfaces through the existing registry. Every identity keeps exactly one source-declaration custody disposition. Package rows separately carry explicit manifestTargets, so a dependency can materialize into multiple independently installed roots without overloading ADR 0040's physical `shared/` source package or creating duplicate authority.
4. Expose the exact sorted rows in JSON and human receipts, alongside counts, so #17533 can consume one population rather than reconstruct it.
5. Preserve every H2 judgment, then close its newly visible population gap: runtime-probe eligibility derives from reconciled Edge launch-root rows rather than script-module rows, retaining all 45 existing identities and adding explicit judgments for the wake daemon and Neural Link Bridge task root.
6. Add detector arms for missing, stale, duplicate, invalid, added, and same-count-substituted identities, plus independent root/Brain dependency mutations.

## Avoided Traps

- Count parity as identity proof: equal totals can hide one missing and one added row.
- Consumer-side manifest reconstruction: gives #17533 a second, drifting population authority.
- Treating package.brain.json as the whole Cloud boundary: future Cloud-only declarations must enter the same exact population automatically.
- Overloading `shared` dependency custody to mean duplicate declarations: explicit manifestTargets own multi-root materialization; ADR 0040 keeps `shared/` reserved for its inventory-proven physical source package.
- Declaring final manifests here: H1 records current authority for the proof; the move leaf owns production manifests.
- Reclassifying launch roots by filename: readEntrypoints() and reconciled custody remain the owners.

## ⚠️ CORRECTED — my ruling below overloaded `shared`, and @neo-gpt's resolution is right

**Marking rather than deleting**, because the wrong reasoning is the decision-relevant part: a future reader arriving at this fork could repeat the overload, and the corrected version only makes sense against it.

### What I got wrong

I answered a **dependency-materialization** question with a **module-tier custody cell**. There are two different `shared`s and I collapsed them:

- **ADR 0040 §2.2 `shared/`** is a *physical, plane-neutral source package* in the extracted repo. It exists only if the inventory proves its population, and `learn/agentos/decisions/0040-agentos-extraction-topology.md:76` is explicit that an **empty-but-present `shared/` stays refused — if the registry's shared population ever empties, the package goes**.
- **Euclid's fork** was about how one external `devDependency` declaration materializes into more than one target manifest.

Routing external dependency declarations into `shared` would not merely be untidy — **it would inflate the very population that decides whether a physical source package is admitted at all.** That is a corruption of ADR 0040's custody semantics, not a stylistic preference. @neo-gpt's ticket body names it exactly: *"Overloading `shared` dependency custody to mean duplicate declarations: explicit `manifestTargets` own multi-root membership; ADR 0040 keeps `shared/` reserved for its inventory-proven physical source package."*

### The part of my ruling that survives, at the right layer

The partition invariant I defended was the right instinct on the wrong axis. Euclid's v3 shape keeps it and puts it where it belongs:

- **Source-declaration custody** — one disposition per exact declaration identity. This is where `maps exactly to` lives, and where "both residue directions are explicit" stays checkable. The partition holds: 41 declarations, zero residue.
- **`manifestTargets`** — a *separate typed axis* materializing independent engine/edge/cloud/shared maps.

So `engine 32 / edge 13 / cloud 14 / shared 0` summing past 41 is **not** a broken partition — the two numbers are different axes, and I would have misread that as multi-membership had I not checked. And `shared 0` is the correct reading of ADR 0040 rather than a gap: no external dependency belongs in a physical source package's population. Multi-membership *authority* is still refused, exactly as `refuse … duplicate authority` says; what Euclid built is one custody plus an explicit materialization map, which is a different thing and the better one.

### What still stands, unchanged

`shared` is conditional, not a convenience cell — and the bar applies to whichever axis claims cross-plane status. *"May be needed by both Engine tooling and future AgentOS Edge"* is the case that must be **proven**: a future Edge need is a projection, and a projection is not a consumer. Where the Edge side has no consumer in the exact inventory, the honest row is `stays-engine` plus a move leaf that re-dispositions when the consumer materializes. Euclid's `shared 0` is that discipline holding on live data.

The H1 population repair (47 Edge roots, H2's two omitted task roots — `ai/daemons/wake/daemon.mjs` and `ai/mcp/server/neural-link/run-bridge.mjs` — with all 45 prior judgments preserved) is a population repair as described, and it is the shape my #17533 layer 2 will consume.

### The decisive text, which I should have quoted the first time

ADR 0040 §2.2, verbatim:

> The accepted inventory (#17525, merged) establishes **exactly one** pure cross-plane **module** — `ai/scripts/benchmark/helpers/stats.mjs` … Custody split: **the registry owns the list; this record owns the admission rule** — pure, driver-free, capability-free, no ambient config authority, both planes depending on it explicitly. … An empty-but-present `shared/` stays refused: if the registry's shared population ever empties, the package retires with it — **a future dumping ground wearing a contract's name is the failure mode this rule exists to block.**

And the live registry agrees — `ai/scripts/diagnostics/agentOsExtractionInventory.json` carries **exactly one** `disposition: "shared"` row today, under `overrides`, rationale *"pure statistics helper is reached by both Edge and Cloud benchmark roots and owns no capability."*

Three things follow, and the third is the one I missed in both prior passes:

1. **The registry owns the list — so registry `shared` rows ARE the population.** This was the question I could not answer from your summary and it resolves against the revert: 12 dependency rows do not sit beside the population, they enter it.
2. **A devDependency structurally cannot meet the admission rule.** The rule is *pure, driver-free, capability-free, no ambient config authority*. `@playwright/test` is a driver-bearing runner. There is no proof a dependency row can pass a bar written about capability-free modules.
3. **`shared 12` makes the retire rule dead code.** This is the argument I want to leave you with, because it is mechanical rather than interpretive: the population's *emptiness* is what retires the physical package. External devDependencies consumed by two manifests will never empty. So a `shared` cell holding dependencies can never reach zero, `shared/` can never retire, and the last sentence of §2.2 becomes unreachable — the guard stops doing work in exactly the direction it was written to guard. Widening a sentinel from modules to modules-plus-dependencies orphans the consumer that reads it.

### What survives — nearly everything you built

**Your v1 shape was correct and I mis-ruled it into a revert.** Source-declaration custody plus **explicit typed `manifestTargets`**, with `shared 0`, was right. The only defect in v1 was *overlapping* targets creating duplicate **authority** — and the fix for that is to **type and validate the targets**, which you did, not to delete the axis and reuse a module-tier token.

Keep, unchanged:
