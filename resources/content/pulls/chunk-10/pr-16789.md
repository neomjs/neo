Resolves #16782

@neo-gpt reopened #16782 against the merged head: the blast-radius arm measured five distinct **leaf** values and called them **principal coordinates**. He is right, and the count is re-derived independently here rather than conceded — the principal coordinate is `providerBaseUrl`, built from AuthService's trailing-slash-stripped local, so `canonical` and `trailingSlash` are one ownership key and the real number is **four**.

The error is the exact conflation this same spec pins one case below. Having found that the leaf and the AuthInfo carry two different spellings, the arm then took the leaf spelling and labelled it the principal coordinate — conflating the two readers whose divergence is the finding. Landing the correction as a follow-up PR because #16783 merged at `13:18:30Z` while I was preparing it.

Evidence: L3 (unit execution against the real `configBase` declarations) → L3 required (the corrected counts are present-tense properties of committed source). Residual: none.

## Deltas from ticket

The reopen's second half — the missing collision control — lands here too, and it is the sharper half. Rather than only lowering 5 to 4, the arm now pins **which** axis collapses and asserts that the other three do not. That makes the witness say something the raw count cannot: normalization here is **partial and axis-inconsistent** rather than absent. Whoever owns the contract inherits three unnormalized axes sitting beside one that is already handled, and a uniform rule would have to move all four.

## What changed

Both layers are measured separately instead of one standing in for the other:

| layer | distinct values | why |
|---|---:|---|
| leaf (`AiConfig.auth.gitlabApiBaseUrl`) | 5 | the leaf normalizes on no axis |
| principal coordinate (`providerBaseUrl`) | **4** | AuthService strips the trailing slash at the use site |

Plus the collision control: `canonical` and `trailingSlash` are asserted **equal** after the strip, while `upperCaseHost`, `defaultPort` and `relativeRoot` are each asserted **distinct** from `canonical`.

The red-proof from the original arm still applies to the leaf count (`toBe(1)` failed with `Received: 5`), so neither count passes vacuously on an env override that never applied.
