Resolves #15233

The deference lexicon now distinguishes the two postures that share surface phrasing via **positive decision-attribution** (sharpened across two review cycles): the exemption requires affirmative evidence that the matched phrase itself attributes a strictly human-owned decision (Tier-4 enumeration — merge execution, credentials, release direction; seed regex `merge/merge-eligible/squash/credential/release/stamp`) to the human. Mechanics: (1) only attribution-shaped phrases (`your call` / `your move` / `Your steer on`) are exemption-eligible — offer-shaped phrases (`would you like me to …`, `want me to …`) propose AGENT execution and always fire, so "Would you like me to merge this PR?" refuses; (2) the DECISIVE segment is the phrase's own attachment — its object segment when present (covering phrase-internal complements like `your steer on the next lane`), else its immediate predicate — with NO wider clause-window fallback, so an outer historical fact ("the merge landed, …") can never lend authority to a neutral attachment; (3) the decisive segment exempts only when it carries all three positive legs — a pure human-domain signal; DECISION-ATTRIBUTION grammar (ownership `your/yours/you`, open-choice `or/whether/either`, or pending-decision `-eligible` — a completed/status-only fact like "The merge landed" hands no decision and fires); and zero negation anywhere in the segment (contractions and modals included, before or after the noun — "Don't merge it", "the merge isn't yours", "should not proceed" all fire). Neutral, unenumerated, competing (a `MAINTAINER_DOMAIN_RE` supplies the other side: lanes/reviews/tickets/designs/epics/discussions/specs/implementations/refactors/backlog), status-only, or negated segments all fire. Ambiguity fails toward firing, mechanically. The check slots in as a third context guard beside the existing reported-mention and attributive-citation carve-outs, and the refusal reminder names the domain-scoping boundary (ticket AC-3, wording aligned to the attachment rule). The live 08:54Z terminal ("merge on the exception or ask for the stamp, your call") remains the pinned regression fixture — its decisive segment ("ask for the stamp") carries the positive evidence.
