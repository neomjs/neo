## What ships

The skill mandated a divergence-window closure it **never defined**. `:68` and `:70` gated the convergence pass on a closure event no line stated, so every author had to invent a trigger — and a wall-clock timestamp is the cheapest and worst one, because no evidence can shorten it.

> After ≥1 substantive non-author cycle the author dispositions **every** live option / falsifier / blocker, then posts **`[DIVERGENCE_FOLDED @ <last-substantive-comment-id>]`**.
