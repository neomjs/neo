## Deltas from ticket

Intake falsified two mechanisms in the original ticket. Retry exhaustion already falls back to the ordinary periodic cadence, and off-host durability posture is already projected at boot and enforced by the backup wrapper. This PR therefore adds no re-arm scheduler, timestamp store, or durability control. It fixes the confirmed five-second dependency-resolve horizon and projects the existing periodic fallback, success age, and maintenance verdict so `phase: exhausted` no longer reads as a permanently dead lane.

The environment variable names and precedence are unchanged; only their shipped fallback values move from 5 attempts / 5 seconds to 10 attempts / 15 seconds.
