## Deltas from ticket

- The terminal “Manage instances…” affordance is intentionally not inserted into the closed `FleetInstance` record schema. `InstanceMenuList` appends one stable menu item after the Store-backed profile rows; every profile row still comes directly from the provider Store with no parallel array or `rows.map(...)` projection.
