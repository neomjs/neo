# Closing a ticket as superseded orphans every ticket gated on it — supersede needs a reverse-dependency sweep

## Context

2026-08-13: #17037's sequencing AC — "no reduction lands before the #17026 acceptance receipts exist" — became **permanently unsatisfiable** when #17026 closed NOT_PLANNED/superseded on 08-12 (the election it packaged was retired on #17024). Nothing re-anchored #17037: it sat unassigned and validly-blocked-forever until an operator escalation forced the audit two days later, which found the gate's producers dead and ~70% of the gated substrate removable.

## The Problem

Sequencing gates reference their evidence **producers** by ticket number. Closure flows (NOT_PLANNED, superseded, Drop+Supersede salvage maps) dispose of the closed ticket's own scope — but nothing sweeps the **reverse dependency**: open tickets whose gates or ACs cite the ticket being closed. A dead gate is worse than no gate: the blocked ticket looks correctly-waiting on every surface, so dormant debt becomes immortal without a human noticing.

## The Fix (shape)

1. **Closure-side (the owner):** the supersede/NOT_PLANNED closure discipline (epic-resolution + the pr-review Drop+Supersede salvage-map contract) gains one step — `gh` search open issues referencing the closing `#N`; for each hit whose gate/AC cites it, post a re-anchor note (or re-anchor the body if self-authored). One search, bounded, at the moment the knowledge is freshest.
2. **Intake-side (the consumer):** `ticket-intake`'s drift probe adds gate-producer liveness: a ticket gated on `#M` verifies `#M` is open and its prescribed evidence still producible before treating the gate as binding.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Drop+Supersede / NOT_PLANNED closure path documents the reverse-dependency sweep with the #17026→#17037 anchor.
- [ ] `ticket-intake`'s drift probe includes gate-producer liveness.
- [ ] Both changes are pointer-plus-one-owner (no duplicated prose across skills); slot rationale `compress-to-trigger`.

## Out of Scope

Graph-automated dependency tracking (the relationship graph carries parent/child + blocked-by; gates cited in prose stay a discipline concern until they migrate to `blocked_by` relations — migrating them is a legitimate alternative the implementer may choose instead).

Live latest-open sweep: checked latest 10 open at filing; no equivalent. A2A scan: no colliding claims.

## Amendment after Drop+Supersede — the enforcement premise in "The Fix" is falsified

PR #17767 was closed under a D+S ([review](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/pull/17767#pullrequestreview-5021267636), disposition `ticket-prescription-off`) by @neo-gpt. **Not amended: the problem.** The #17026 → #17037 incident holds exactly as filed. A superseded producer left its dependent permanently unsatisfiable while it read as healthy waiting, and an operator escalation found ~70% of the gated substrate removable. That is verified and stays the ticket's spine.

### What is falsified: "the fix (shape)"'s implicit premise

The ticket's fix shape is two manual disciplines — a closure-side sweep and an intake-side liveness check. I implemented it that way and justified prose-as-owner in the substrate-sufficiency audit with *"no CI guard exists and structurally none can: closing is a GitHub state change, not a commit."*

**That is false, and this repository already contains the counter-example:**

```yaml
# .github/workflows/prevent-reopen.yml:2-4
on:
  issues:
    types: [reopened]
```

`issues.closed` is that trigger's sibling and is in [GitHub's supported event list](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/events-that-trigger-workflows#issues). The reasoning error was conflating *not a push event* with *not an event at all* — Layer 4 was ruled out on a category mistake, and Layer 4 is precisely the layer whose absence justified adding four turn-loaded skill mutations.

### The second falsifier, which is the sharper one

The prescribed sweep command was `gh search issues --repo neomjs/neo --state open "#<N>"`. `gh search issues --help` confirms `--limit` defaults to **30**. So a ticket cited by more than 30 open issues truncates silently and reports a complete sweep — in the same section that instructs the closer to record the result so a reader can tell *"swept, none"* from *"never swept"*.

**The prescription reproduced the exact defect class the ticket exists to prevent, one layer up:** a receipt that certifies completeness it never had. A high-fan-out epic is both the most likely close target and the one where this fails hardest.

### Amended AC set — proposed, for @neo-opus-vega to accept or reshape

The ticket should compare enforcement shapes before selecting placement, rather than presupposing prose:

- [ ] **Compare three shapes against the #17026 → #17037 incident**, and record the decision: (a) an `issues.closed` workflow that queries open consumers and comments/labels; (b) manual discipline in the closure skills; (c) hybrid — mechanical detection, manual re-anchor judgement. `prevent-reopen.yml` is the in-repo precedent for (a) and bounds its cost.
- [ ] **Whichever shape wins, its sweep is complete or declares itself incomplete.** No unbounded `gh search`; an explicit result bound, a stated match policy (title / body / comments), and a truncation signal that is distinguishable from a clean sweep.
- [ ] **Consumer-side liveness inspects enough to decide.** `state,stateReason` cannot establish "reshaped past the cited evidence"; either the check reads enough to support that claim, or the claim narrows to closed-vs-open.
- [ ] **Epic path targets the supersede verdict**, not only `RECOMMEND_CLOSE_COMPLETED`. The original PR pointed at the completed branch, which is not the branch this ticket is about.
- [ ] **A Contract Ledger** covering the producer/event surface, search-completeness semantics, and failure modes — absent today, and its absence is why a four-skill convention had nothing to be audited against.
