Resolves #17094

Stops the real-process Neural Link stdio witness from killing unrelated unit CI after its child transport closes. The handshake now consumes each complete stdout frame once, serializes its protocol writes through an exit-safe boundary, owns child and stdin lifecycle errors, and settles as unavailable instead of leaking `EPIPE` outside Playwright.

Evidence: L2 (focused unit coverage plus 50 repeated real child-process handshakes) → L2 required. Residual: none.
