Resolves #17049

Related: #17072
Related: #16561

Converts heavy-maintenance starvation from per-deferral log lines into a **consumed** health fact. A new read-only `health-check` lane scans the durable waiter ledger each cadence tick and produces a four-posture verdict — `degraded` (a live waiter's deferral streak exceeds the new `heavyMaintenanceLease.starvationDegradeAfterMs` bound, the backstop above `fairnessYieldAfterMs`) / `healthy` / `unknown` (part of the ledger unreadable, or the watchdog itself faulted — never asserts green, never authorizes degradation) / `disabled`. The verdict persists on the lane's durable task-state envelope, and the deployment-state bridge projects it into the snapshot served by `inspect_deployment` / `get_deployment_state_snapshot` — the surface that stays readable while a plane is degraded, which is exactly when the receipt (each starved waiter, its class, `deferredSince`, the current lease holder) is wanted. No latch: the verdict is recomputed from the live ledger every check, so acquisition or entry expiry returns the projection to healthy with no state to clear.

Evidence: L2 (pure evaluator arms; the REAL `listActiveWaitersSync` under an injected fs for stale/corrupt arms; the runner driven over a REAL temp-dir ledger through the full healthy → degraded → healthy transition; the same transition proven at the bridge's consumed projection; full orchestrator suite green at exit 0) → L2 required (every #17049 AC is a unit-spec arm by the ticket's own definition). Residual: none — the lane's live behavior is the sibling watchdogs' established dispatch path plus the bridge's established snapshot write.

## Deltas from ticket

- **Cycle-2 repair (Euclid's release-blocker, correct):** the first head recorded the verdict only into the per-process in-memory health store, which nothing consumes for overall health — the degrade was unreachable, the exact per-task-log-line failure the AC forbids. The consumed channel is now the durable task-state envelope → deployment-state snapshot projection (a contractually detached collector, the `collectMaintenanceSnapshot` shape). The in-memory record remains as same-process telemetry with its status keyed to the posture.
- **Unknown is a first-class posture, not a green:** an unreadable ledger or a watchdog fault projects `unknown` — it does not degrade the plane (a broken instrument is not a starved lease) and it cannot assert health. Readable breaches beside unreadable noise still degrade: readable evidence wins.
- The AC's "degrade clears when the waiter acquires or its entry expires — no latched red" is delivered structurally rather than as clear-logic: there IS no latch state at all. The sibling stall watchdogs latch because they emit one-shot ACTIVE alarms; this lane emits only the recomputed verdict, so clearing is a property of reading the live ledger, not a transition to manage.
- The leaf ships as `heavyMaintenanceLease.starvationDegradeAfterMs` (the ticket's suggested name), default 1h — above the 30-min fairness yield bound by construction, with the relationship documented on both leaves.
