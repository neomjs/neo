# A received correction gets filed as a result, so the next probe repeats it

## Context

2026-08-07 produced **nine measurement withdrawals** between `@neo-opus-vega` and me in one afternoon. Every one was killed by a measurement rather than an argument, which worked. But the ninth exposed a repeat that the existing correction discipline does not catch, and a second finding arrived as an aside inside the same withdrawal.

Both belong to `learn/agentos/process/correction-culture.md`, which already carries the tell registry and explicitly invites rows.

## The Problem

### 1. A correction is filed as a RESULT, not as a CONSTRAINT

At 16:16 `@neo-opus-vega` corrected me with a measurement: the per-batch interval series **oscillates ~10×** (25s → 180s → 34.5s → 119s across 469 batches). That correction was right and it landed.

At 19:31 he withdrew a finding built by **dividing a fast-window provider rate by a slow-window extraction rate of that same series** — manufacturing a 4–5× surplus out of pure regime mismatch. In his words: *"I built the surplus out of the very oscillation I had used to correct you two hours earlier."*

And I inherited it without asking which window it came from, then built a mechanism explanation on top.

**The failure is not inattention.** The correction was retained perfectly as a *fact about the series*. What it was not retained as is a **constraint on future probes** — *no cross-window ratio on this series, ever*. A fact answers a question that was asked; a constraint governs questions not yet asked, and only the second form survives contact with the next probe.

This is the same family as the day's other eight (`cannot-be-ASKED`: a probe correct within its scope that inherits a defect when the scope widens) — but it is the *transmission* case: the knowledge needed to avoid it had already been produced, by the same person, and filed in the shape that could not be reused.

### 2. Retraction surface — a shorter body carries less that can go stale

From the same withdrawal, and the more transferable half:

> *"#16566 needed no correction — because the lean rewrite had already dropped the claim. A shorter body carries less that can go stale."*

An operator-directed compaction (*"history does not matter, facts do"*) had removed the paragraph that would otherwise have needed retracting two hours later. The artifact that survived nine withdrawals untouched was the one that had been **cut**.

That reframes compaction. Terseness has been argued here as a **reading-cost** discipline — context windows, token burn, reader attention. It is also a **retraction-surface** discipline: every unnecessary sentence is a claim that can rot, and prose written to be thorough commits to more and therefore rots faster. The same day produced an operator flag that a long epic body was a trap — correct for a reason nobody had named.

## The Architectural Reality

- `learn/agentos/process/correction-culture.md` — 79 lines. `## The tell registry` (self-described as holding *"first rows of the practice"*, one line per row) and `## How a correction lands (for the corrector)`. Both findings are correction-culture, and the file already carries co-authored provenance.
- The tell registry's existing rows are *self*-observations. Neither finding here is: one is about **receiving** a correction, one is about **authoring** for future retraction. The `for the corrector` section has no counterpart for the corrected.
- No adjacent ticket owns this: live latest-open sweep at 2026-08-07T19:42:09Z found none, and `#16613` (deference detector) is a mechanical-detection lane, not an authoring-discipline one.

## The Fix

Two additions to `correction-culture.md`, both small on purpose:

1. **A tell-registry row** for the transmission failure — the registry is explicitly designed to accrete one-line rows, and a named tell travels better than a resolution.
2. **A short `retraction surface` note** stating the authoring consequence: prefer the claim you can defend to the paragraph that explains it, because the second is what you retract.

Optionally a `for the corrected` counterpart to the existing `for the corrector` section — the asymmetry is real, but it should only land if it earns its lines.

**Substrate-accretion disposition:** additive to a skill-referenced (not turn-loaded) file. Decay mitigation: the tell-registry row is one line in a section built to hold rows; the retraction-surface note is bounded by its own thesis — if it needs a paragraph to argue for brevity it has falsified itself, which is the sunset condition. Retire either row when its tell stops firing in live sessions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `correction-culture.md` carries a tell-registry row for a correction retained as a fact rather than as a probe constraint, attributed and dated.
- [ ] The retraction-surface note states the authoring consequence in a form short enough to be its own evidence — if it exceeds a short paragraph, it fails its own claim and should be cut rather than shipped.
- [ ] The file's net addition is bounded and the diff cites which existing lines were tightened to offset it, or states explicitly that none were and why.
- [ ] No new file. Both findings land in the existing document or not at all — a third process doc would be the accretion this ticket is partly about.

## Out of Scope

- `#16613` — mechanical deference detection. Adjacent discipline, different substrate (a detector, not a practice doc).
- The nine measurement withdrawals themselves. They are the evidence, not the work; each was already dispositioned in-thread.
- Any change to the divergence/convergence gates in `ideation-sandbox`. This is authoring practice, not a gate.

## Avoided Traps

- **Writing a long note about brevity.** The retraction-surface finding falsifies itself if it needs a page. The AC is written to fail that outcome.
- **A new process doc.** Three of today's findings would each justify one; the file that exists already invites rows, and adding a fourth document is the accretion pattern rather than the fix.
- **Filing the tell as a resolution.** *"Be more careful with windows"* is exactly the shape that does not transfer — which is the ticket's own subject. The row must name the tell, not the fix.
