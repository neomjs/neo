# Workspace continuity guard is host-scoped and its red-control has no call sites

## Context

Found by @neo-opus-grace while driving `#16498` (A2A, 2026-08-04 ~15:16Z), on witness infrastructure I authored: the five-beat suite's `assertWorkspaceContinuity` guard. She proved both halves empirically before reporting; filed by the instrument's author. Live latest-open sweep at filing: latest 20 checked, no equivalent.

## The Problem

Two compounding instrument defects make the continuity guard's green mean less than it appears across most of the film-audit set:

1. **Magnitude mismatch (host-scoped entropy vs pane-scoped defects).** The guard measures Shannon entropy over the ENTIRE dock host (`page.locator('.workstation-dock-host').boundingBox()` — all twenty panes) against a floor of `baselineEntropy * 0.65`. A single pane's card body going fully black is a small fraction of those pixels and cannot pull whole-host entropy down 35%. Grace's measured film-paced run: floor `3.55`, measured `4.95` — a margin that means nothing for pane-scale defects because the signal never enters the measurement. The guard is correctly sized for whole-body clears (`#16500`'s class) and structurally blind to single-pane clears (`#16498`'s class; plausibly `#16497`/`#16499`'s pane-scoped symptoms too).

2. **The red-control direction is dead code.** `expectedCleared` — the guard's known-defect direction, written so the instrument could prove it can fail — has ZERO call sites outside `assertWorkspaceContinuity` itself (`grep -rn 'expectedCleared' test/playwright/e2e/` → 3 hits, all internal). Written, branched on, never exercised: the guard has never demonstrated it can go red in the direction it exists to catch.

## The Architectural Reality

- `test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationFiveBeatNL.spec.mjs` — the box read (`:948`-region) and the floor (`:1146`-region, `* 0.65`), plus the `expectedCleared` branch inside `assertWorkspaceContinuity`.
- Calibration asset now available: the take-18 F7 band (real single-pane clear on camera) can be run through the whole-host measure to quantify exactly how little a pane-scale defect moves it — either calibrating a pane-scoped floor or proving the measurement must be pane-scoped (Grace's current direction under `#16498`: the audit pane's own region + `vdom.removeDom`).

## The Fix

- Add pane-scoped measurement capability to the continuity instrument (per-region entropy or per-pane box sampling), sized so a single-pane clear is detectable; keep the host-scoped measure for whole-body classes.
- Exercise `expectedCleared` with at least one real call site (a deliberate known-clear fixture), or remove the branch — an unexercised red-control is instrument theater.
- Calibrate against the take-18 F7 band (real defective frames) so both floors trace to measured truth rather than chosen constants.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A staged single-pane clear turns the instrument red; the same stage pre-fix (host-scoped only) stays green — the blind spot demonstrated red/green.
- [ ] `expectedCleared` (or its successor) has a real exercised call site, or is removed with rationale.
- [ ] Floors trace to measurements (the F7-band calibration or equivalent), documented in-spec.
- [ ] Existing five-beat suite stays green spec-paced and film-paced.
