# A cleared grid pool row retains worker-side record identity

## Context

PR #17523 surfaced a useful invariant while attempting to resolve #17427: a pooled row whose worker-side `record` is `null` should not retain the prior record's identity in its worker-side VDOM.

That is smaller than #17427. Both the `recordId` deletion and `display: none` are staged under `silent: true` and depend on the same `Body#createViewData` trailing update to reach the browser. If that flush is lost, neither mutation changes the painted DOM. This ticket therefore owns only the worker-state invariant; #17427 retains the flush/delivery defect.

Live latest-open sweep: checked the latest 20 open issues and the 30 most recent all-state A2A messages at 2026-08-22T00:48:39.433Z; no equivalent narrow ticket or in-flight claim was found. #17427 is related but intentionally owns the painted-DOM flush boundary.

## The Problem

`Row#createVdom` clears a pool slot by setting `record: null`, but the pre-fix VDOM retains `data.recordId` from the record that previously occupied that slot. Worker-side inspection can therefore observe contradictory truth: `row.record === null` while `row.vdom.data.recordId` still names a record.

That mismatch is independently wrong even when the eventual browser update hides the row. It misleads worker-plane diagnostics and makes later reasoning about painted versus staged state harder.

It is not, by itself, the cause or repair for a painted duplicate when the trailing update is lost. A silent VDOM mutation has no independent browser delivery path.

## The Architectural Reality

- `src/grid/Body.mjs:createViewData` clears unused pool slots through `item.updateContent({record: null, rowIndex: -1, silent: true})` and performs one trailing `update()` when the outer call is not silent.
- `src/grid/Row.mjs:createVdom` mutates the worker-side VDOM. Under `silent: true`, those mutations are staged; they do not directly update the physical DOM.
- `data.recordId` identifies the record claim. `data.rowId` identifies the reusable pool slot and remains valid after the record is cleared.

## The Fix

When `Row#createVdom` sees no record, remove only `vdom.data.recordId`, retain `vdom.data.rowId`, stage `display: none`, and preserve the existing update/batching behavior.

The permanent test must assert worker-side VDOM truth and its inverse control. It must not describe that assertion as painted-DOM delivery.

## Out of Scope

- Resolving #17427's flush-bound derivation, AC-4, or AC-5.
- Changing row pooling, slot assignment, or delta depth.

## Avoided Traps

**Calling worker VDOM painted truth.** A silent mutation is not a browser update; the outer flush remains the delivery boundary.

**Deleting the whole data object.** `rowId` still identifies the pool slot, and removing it changes the guarded delta shape.

**Closing the broader flush ticket with a staged-state invariant.** #17427 remains the authority for the unresolved painted-DOM defect.
