## Deltas from ticket

- **Envelope field names verbatim, not the contract sketch's:** the leg-2 contract sketched `{subscriptionId, envelopeDigest, kind, sentAt, receivedAt}`; the landed model reads the signed envelope's OWN grammar (`eventId`, `eventType` as `kind`, `logId`, `emittedAt`) plus the client `receivedAt` stamp — "nothing re-derived" argued for verbatim names once the real grammar was in view.
- **Degraded speaks through the telltale, not a SpineBanner extension:** the contract's "degraded speaks through the existing SpineBanner" collided with the banner module's own law ("a verdict may only speak for the surface that produced it"; per-agent truth is explicitly a different surface — per-VIEWER truth is too). A dead push lane is comfort loss, not spine degradation (push is latency, poll is truth), so the telltale owns the full tri-state with the consumer's reason, reusing the banner's token-indirection skin idiom, and no new alarm surface exists. The e2e pins the independence: spine degraded while MY push lane is live.
- **The pollDigest seam is honestly injection-only in the browser:** build-time discovery — `poll-digest` is an authenticated MC-plane call, the mints live in bridge closures, and the composed server exposes no viewer poll surface; so the cockpit's `wakePollDigest` seam passes through when a composition supplies one and renders honest absence otherwise. The server-brokered surface (which lights the seam up with zero client change) is #17252.
- **Consumer + capability extension:** leg-1's consumer gains `onWake` (observer, absorbed-fault, validated) and `lastCatchUp` (timestamped tri-state); `openWakeStream`'s observational whitelist adds exactly `onWake`. `formatAge` is exported from `agentFreshness` (one relative-age formatter, two chrome consumers — no duplicate).
