Cut 2 of the navigation model — the tab ANATOMY: one §04 chrome-tier family for every tab tier. Keeper rail, south content strips, and the detail's inner strip speak the chrome role (11px/1 mono, `.08em`, uppercase) with a structural active state (signal indicator + ink + weight, never hue-alone); the dock rail joins the family's VOICE and hover idiom only — its ordinary buttons carry no pressed indicator, deliberately: auto-hidden reveals are invocations, not a persistent selection. Cut 1 (document + presets) and the bar de-crowd landed earlier; AC-1's model was operator-approved on the ticket.
