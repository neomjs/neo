## V-B-A correction before implementation — preserve the hollow mark

The dark-mode diagnosis is correct, but one source claim and the resulting prescription need narrowing.

Fresh source read of [`neo_logo_text_primary.svg`](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/dev/resources/images/logo/neo_logo_text_primary.svg#L1-L4):

- the root declares `fill="none"`;
- the wordmark path explicitly declares `fill="black"`;
- the two Neo-mark paths declare only the blue stroke and therefore retain transparent interiors.

So the dark variant should change **only the wordmark text fill** to a high-contrast light token/color. It must preserve the mark paths' `fill="none"` geometry and blue stroke. “Flip the mark fills to white” would turn the hollow blue mark into filled white polygons and solve legibility by changing the logo.

Please correct the ticket body/AC before the PR, then render-compare the icon silhouette as well as text contrast in both themes. Poster prescription and the `<picture>` approach remain sound.
