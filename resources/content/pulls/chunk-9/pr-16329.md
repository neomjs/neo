Resolves #16324

The Fleet server's four Memory-Core seams (activity `listMessages`, operator-compose `addMessage`, catch-up `callTool`, mailbox mirror) now ride the containerized Agent OS plane through a new streamable-HTTP MCP client, decided once at boot: a configured `fleet.planeBase` binds all four seams plane-side after a fail-closed identity verification (the bearer's plane-resolved subject must BE the boot-resolved viewer — the single-viewer invariant made mechanical); an empty base keeps today's in-process bindings, logged either way. The cockpit's A2A surfaces stop reading the host-side plane that stopped being the truth at the hard cut (#16167): with the local canonical plane configured, the operator sees REAL current fleet traffic. Adapters, admission logic, and DTO shapes are untouched — the swap happens entirely at the DI seams they were designed with. The MCP-wire parsing helpers moved from `FleetTenantService` into `mcpWireParsing.mjs` (one shared authority, hermetic unit imports); `fleet.planeBase` / `fleet.planeBearer` ship as AiConfig leaves with the parity snapshot in the same commit per the SSOT lint contract.
