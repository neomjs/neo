Resolves #17241

Related: #17522 · #17211 · #17514

🌿 *The engine had no splitter paint at all, so a consumer who adopted the dock got an invisible drag target.*

Evidence: L2 (compiled CSS inspected, source-invariant guard, three mutations) → L2 required (build-time styling, no deployed surface). No residual.

## The defect

`.neo-dashboard-dock-splitter` in the engine carried `flex-shrink`, `position`, `touch-action`, `z-index` and a hit-target `::before`. **No paint.** Every visible pixel lived in `Workspace.scss` and `FleetCockpit.scss`, so `examples/dashboard/dock` — a consumer doing exactly what the docs say — got a drag target it could not see. That is a reported defect, not a neutral default.

## The design, and it is not mine

@neo-fable's answer, recorded on the ticket rather than left in A2A. **Option 3: the engine ships a discoverable affordance; an app opts out by token.**

> Discoverability is the engine's duty; aesthetics are the app's.

Her refinement corrected my reading rather than nuancing it. I had the workstation splitter as **one** design to tokenise. It is **two**:

| layer | owner | content |
|---|---|---|
| structure | engine, untokened | `flex-shrink`, `position`, `touch-action`, `z-index`, hit-target `::before` |
| **affordance floor** | engine, **token defaults** | band, 36×2 centred handle, plain hover, functional active |
| identity | app, **token values** | workstation ring + glow; FM `handle-size: 0` |

**The pill ring and outer glow are not in the floor.** They were born reading `--workstation-signal` — consumer signal-language. Shipping them as the engine default would push one app's identity onto every other consumer, which is this promotion's own defect pointed the other way. They default to `none`; workstation re-adds them by value.

Defaults mix against `currentColor`: the engine cannot know a host palette, and a literal grey reads wrong on half of them.

## FM's flat splitter becomes a statement, not an absence

`--dock-splitter-handle-size: 0`. It greps, and it survives the engine gaining a handle — which is what just happened. Whether FM *keeps* flat now that a discoverable handle exists is @neo-fable-clio's call on FM's design language; the engine answer does not depend on it.

## The `!important` is gone, not moved

Workstation's active state carried `opacity: 1 !important`. Fable named it as a debt to refuse at promotion time, and she was right: it meant a competing rule was being fought rather than resolved. The engine now owns the active state at its own specificity, so the conflict does not exist at this layer.

Carrying it across would have laundered a specificity defect into the engine **under a no-visual-change banner** — and preserving behaviour exactly is precisely the instinct that would have done it. A guard arm now fails on any `!important` in the promoted rule.

## Test Evidence

`dockSplitterLayering.spec.mjs` — 5 arms. `buildScripts` **136 passed**; `test-components` **50 passed** (real rendering).

Verified on **compiled** CSS: the engine emits the floor with `var()` indirection, both apps emit only custom properties.

**Three mutations, each reddening its own arm:**

| mutation | reddens |
|---|---|
| an app re-paints the splitter | the layering arm — `FleetCockpit.scss → background on .neo-dashboard-dock-splitter` |
| the engine ships a ring by default | the identity-slot arm — identity leaking into the floor |
| `!important` in the promoted rule | its own arm |

The depth-scan in the guard is deliberate and carries a comment saying why: the obvious `/[^{}]*\{[^{}]*\}/` nested-block strip also swallows the declarations *preceding* a nested block, so a `background` above an `&:hover { … }` vanishes before inspection. A guard written that way passes its own mutation — that happened on #17522 and the fix travelled here.

## Deltas from ticket

- **The AC's visual-baseline instrument was replaced before this PR started.** The visual suite is a local-harness, platform-specific tool (`FleetCockpitVisual.spec.mjs:27`) whose goldens are not mine to judge; the AC now reads computed/compiled equality, which asserts the property rather than a rendering of it.
- **The floor was hoisted above the nested rules.** Sass emits trailing declarations as a second same-selector block — valid, behaviourally invisible, and confusing in built output.
- **Workstation's per-axis `::after` sizing was deleted, not moved.** The engine's tokens (`handle-size` / `handle-thickness`) now serve both orientations, so the app copies were redundant.

## Out of Scope

- Whether FM should keep a flat splitter — FM design language, @neo-fable-clio's call.
- The rail-tab half, which landed as #17522 / PR #17524.

## Post-Merge Validation

None gating. Both apps resolve to their previous appearance through token values; the engine gains a default no current consumer relied on, and `examples/dashboard/dock` gains a visible one it previously lacked.
