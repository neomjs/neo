# Film-paced resize presents a cleared dense workspace body

## Context

The `WorkstationFiveBeatNL` scene-1 continuity test ("the room is alive: resize and theme preserve continuity") carries an entropy oracle: `scene-1-run-1-resize must not present a cleared dense workspace body`. In film mode (`NEO_FILM_TAKE=1`, D-010 pacing) that oracle went RED **2-for-2** on 2026-08-04 at dev head `2cd55fdd7d` — on two different displays (main + isolated capture display), `minEntropy 0.41` vs the required `≥ 3.48` — while the same test is GREEN 5+-for-5 spec-paced (headless and headed) at the same head. A blank/cleared dense body during resize is a frame the film cannot ship, and the pacing-dependence means ordinary CI never sees it. Sweeps: live latest-20 open (2026-08-04 ~12:15Z) no equivalent; A2A last-30 no claim; local mirror grep for resize-blank: zero hits.

## The Problem

Under film pacing the resize beat presents at least one captured frame whose workspace body is effectively cleared (entropy 0.41 ≈ flat fill). The 6fps take frames around the resize band also show the boot presenting a fully blank dark window before first paint (a separate cold-boot cosmetic, noted, not this ticket's subject) — the oracle red fires DURING the post-boot resize sequence, so the cleared body is a resize-time presentation gap, not the boot blank.

## The Architectural Reality

- Film pacing changes gesture cadence (slow continuous resize with dwells) — the failure being pacing-dependent points at a reflow/paint gap that spec-paced resizes step over: candidates include the ResizeObserver dispatch dam interaction (the `#16402` family repaired hidden-pane starvation; a film-paced live resize is the visible-cadence sibling), FLIP staging of grid/pane content during continuous resize, and worker-truth vs painted-truth divergence windows during long resize gestures.
- The oracle and its screenshot instrument live in the spec (`test/playwright/e2e/workstation/WorkstationFiveBeatNL.spec.mjs`, scene 1); Playwright traces + error context for the two red runs archive with the standard test artifacts.
- Deterministic reproduction: `NEO_E2E_PORT=<port> NEO_FILM_TAKE=1 npx playwright test workstation/WorkstationFiveBeatNL -c test/playwright/playwright.config.e2e.mjs --workers=1 --headed --grep "scene 1"`.

## The Fix

Classification first: capture the cleared frame plus the concurrent worker truth (grid/pane state) and DOM — decide whether the body is unpainted (paint gap), cleared-by-projection (content briefly absent), or a compositing artifact. Repair at the owning seam; the entropy oracle already exists as the witness and must go green ×3 consecutive film-paced runs.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The cleared frame's owner is named with a frame + worker-truth + DOM triplet receipt.
- [ ] Scene-1 film-paced runs green ×3 consecutive on the repaired head (both plain-headed and isolated-display staging).
- [ ] Spec-paced runs stay green (no oracle loosening — the fix is presentation truth, not threshold surgery).
- [ ] If the boot-blank cold-start frame turns out to share the owner, note it; otherwise leave it explicitly out of scope.

## Out of Scope

- Cold-boot first-paint blanking (cosmetic, camera-only — trimmed in film composition).
- Conversion/re-entry defects (`#16497`, `#16499`) and the empty sibling pane (`#16498`).
