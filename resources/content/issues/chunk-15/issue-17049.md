# A starving heavy-maintenance waiter must degrade the orchestrator health surface

## Context

Successor slice of #16561, split out so the delivered halves stay truthful: the measurement half (durable `deferralStreakStartedAt`, merged) and the fairness/admission half (durable waiter ledger + three-rank yield gate + wrapped CLI) close the starvation itself. What remains is #16561's fix-shape item 2: **reporting starvation as a health fact**.

Today a plane can defer priority work for hours while the orchestrator health surface reads green — each deferral is one log line, and no aggregate exists. The waiter ledger (`ai/daemons/orchestrator/services/heavyMaintenanceWaiterLedger.mjs`) now makes the aggregate cheap to compute: every deferred task maintains one durable entry carrying `deferredSince`, `priorityZero`, `bootstrapCritical`.

## AC

- The orchestrator health surface exposes a heavy-maintenance starvation fact derived from the waiter ledger: any live waiter whose streak exceeds a risk bound degrades overall health (not merely a per-task log line), with a receipt naming the waiter, its class, `deferredSince`, and the current lease holder.
- The risk bound is an AiConfig leaf (`heavyMaintenanceLease.starvationDegradeAfterMs` or equivalent) introduced **in the same PR as its consumer** — it was deliberately removed from the fairness PR to avoid shipping dead config.
- The degrade clears when the waiter acquires or its entry expires — no latched red.
- Unit spec arms cover: degrade fires past the bound, clears on acquisition, ignores stale entries, and never throws on a corrupt ledger (fail-open to green with a logged warning, consistent with the admission gate's fail-open posture).

## Non-Goals

- No scheduling/admission changes — the fairness gate owns those.
- No lowering of `staleAfterMs` (unchanged rationale from #16561).
