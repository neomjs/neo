## Deltas from ticket

The ticket allowed split fields or a role parameter. This uses explicit fields and introduces `BUNDLE_INCOMPLETE` so known-incomplete newest history can fall through to an older complete bundle. The recovery-substrate census moved to `bundleIntegrity.mjs` and remains re-exported from `backup.mjs`, keeping producer and consumers on one authority. `restorable` remains a compatibility alias of recovery authorization only.
