Resolves #16750

A Node service that exhausts its declared `--max-old-space-size` already produced an authoritative `container-down` fact, already classified as `crash`, and was already restarted. **The action was never wrong.** What was missing is the CAUSE: nothing read the exit code, so the death recorded as a generic crash, the ceiling was never implicated, and the same abort recurred indefinitely. That is why nothing looked broken from the outside — the heal succeeded every time.

Evidence: L2 (unit + mutation proof; every AC is verifiable in-sandbox because the classifier is a pure function and the reason is asserted through the public `diagnose()` surface). No sandbox-unreachable residual for the ACs — the live-plane observation of a real heap abort is post-merge validation, not an unmet AC.

## It is a CANDIDATE, never a verdict

Exit `134` is `128 + SIGABRT` — shared by every V8 `FATAL ERROR`, every failed assertion and every explicit `abort()`. It names the **manner** of death, never its cause. The discriminating evidence is the stderr `heap limit` line, and `inspect.State` does not carry it.

Naming a verdict the payload cannot support is the exact defect this diagnosis path exists to remove, so the candidate travels with its limits and a consumer may not promote it. Confirming it needs a log-read capability over the Docker socket — deliberately out of scope.

**Tri-state is load-bearing.** `null` means the deciding fields were not observed and must read as absence of signal, never as a negative: a missing `ExitCode` is not evidence of a healthy exit. And `OOMKilled: true` returns `false` **even at 134** — a cgroup kill reclaimed the whole container, so the V8 ceiling is not implicated.

## Why this is not a second heap diagnosis

`#16630`'s Out of scope forbids "a second heap diagnosis kind beside `memory-saturation` — one signal needs the right numerator, not a sibling free to disagree." This is an **enrichment of an existing authoritative fact**: the `container-down` fact already fired and already carried `exitCode`; only the cause was unread. That line stands unamended, and no action class is added, removed or re-routed.
