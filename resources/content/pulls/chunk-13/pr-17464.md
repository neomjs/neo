The cockpit bar now seats exactly the navigation model's roster — presets(+error) · spine banner · Reconnect · wake telltale · fleet-start summary · Start fleet — and the two pane verbs moved home. The mechanism keeps every existing contract: the toggles stay SHELL-owned configs (built by `FleetCockpit`, handlers bound there, labels synced by `syncControlBar`) and land through a new layout-blind `shellTools` slot on `AgentDetail` (identity-header trailing edge) and `MemoriesPane` (actions row) — the panes place, never inspect.
