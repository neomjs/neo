# Prove macOS tear-out terminal cleanup matrix row

## Context

This is the executable row-7 child of #15551 under portability parent #15243 and epic #15239.

The parent intake was narrowed after live evidence changed its premise:

- PR #15552 already shipped runner provenance and the complete macOS row-5 fallback receipt.
- PR #15573 shipped the real Demo B vessel embodiment and lifecycle composition.
- Rows 4 and 6 remain honest architecture holes: row 4 still lacks a real `Neo.data.Store` continuity witness; row 6 still lacks three registered claim targets.

This child owns **only macOS row 7 — terminal cleanup**.

## The Problem

The living portability ledger still marks row 7 `NOT_YET_MEASURED`. Unit suites prove individual outcome machines, but the headed product composition has no one receipt that drives all three required terminals through Demo B and proves the complete cleanup census:

1. committed drop;
2. cancellation after vessel birth;
3. blocked acquisition before vessel birth.

Without that receipt, isolated green reducers cannot establish that the composed browser, worker, native-window, admission, embodiment, and committed-model surfaces clean exactly once together.

## The Architectural Reality

The production seams already exist and are merged:

- `DemoBWorkspace.executeTearOutStep()` drives the real pointer/boundary grammar.
- `openTearOutVessel()` treats a falsy `Neo.Main.windowOpen()` result as fail-closed admission and returns `null`.
- `cancelTearOutGesture()`, `onWindowDisconnect()`, and the merged tear-out outcome/retirement machines own terminal cleanup.
- The observable residue surfaces are the committed dock document, live pane identity, physical popup census, `tearOutConnects`, `tearOutPanes`, `tearOutPlacements`, pending admissions, owner grants, retirement state, and the terminal machine's active vessel.

The blocked-acquisition control belongs at the real browser acquisition boundary: a test-owned `globalThis.open` wrapper returns `null` for the one tear-out target while preserving unrelated opens. It must not replace the product reducer, synthesize a terminal, or add a production test switch.

## The Fix

Extend the existing headed matrix witness family and its living ledger only.

| Terminal | Real stimulus | Required terminal truth |
|---|---|---|
| committed drop | real tab drag across the native boundary and mouseup | one detach commit; one live pane owned by the vessel; gesture residue retired |
| cancel | real boundary exit, real vessel birth, Escape before mouseup | committed document unchanged; vessel retired; same pane restored; zero lifecycle residue |
| blocked acquisition | real boundary gesture with the exact browser open returning `null` | no vessel birth, no model mutation, same live pane/home, zero admission or ownership residue |

For every branch, snapshot all observable cleanup surfaces before and after the first terminal. Deliver the terminal signal a second time and require the complete snapshot to remain byte-equivalent.

Fold only macOS row 7 to `PASS` when all acceptance criteria and universal invariants pass. Rows 4 and 6 remain `NOT_YET_MEASURED`.
