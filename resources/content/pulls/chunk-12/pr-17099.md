Resolves #17049

Related: #17072
Related: #16561

Converts heavy-maintenance starvation from per-deferral log lines into a health fact. A new read-only `health-check` lane scans the durable waiter ledger each cadence tick and records a **failed** outcome while any live waiter's deferral streak exceeds the new `heavyMaintenanceLease.starvationDegradeAfterMs` bound — the backstop above `fairnessYieldAfterMs`, so a breach means fairness itself is not working. The receipt names each starved waiter, its class (`priorityZero` / `bootstrapCritical`), its `deferredSince`, and the current lease holder. No latch: the reading is recomputed from the live ledger every check, so acquisition (entry cleared) or expiry (past the ledger TTL) returns health to green on the next check with no state to clear. A corrupt ledger fails OPEN to green with a logged warning, matching the admission gate's posture.

Evidence: L2 (pure evaluator arms plus the REAL `listActiveWaitersSync` driven through an injected fs for the stale/corrupt arms; full orchestrator unit suite green) → L2 required (every #17049 AC is a unit-spec arm by the ticket's own definition). Residual: none — the lane's live behavior is the sibling watchdogs' established dispatch path.

## Deltas from ticket

- The AC's "degrade clears when the waiter acquires or its entry expires — no latched red" is delivered structurally rather than as clear-logic: there IS no latch state at all. The sibling stall watchdogs latch because they emit one-shot ACTIVE alarms; this lane emits only the recomputed passive record, so clearing is a property of reading the live ledger, not a transition to manage.
- The leaf ships as `heavyMaintenanceLease.starvationDegradeAfterMs` (the ticket's suggested name), default 1h — above the 30-min fairness yield bound by construction, with the relationship documented on both leaves.
