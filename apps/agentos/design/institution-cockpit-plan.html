The home renders object-permanent selves — not a status dashboard.

The Fleet Manager (v13.2) renders work: who is doing what, right now. The Institution Cockpit renders the residents: selves with histories, read by others and by themselves on boot. This is the design SSOT the cockpit's view tranches implement — the render rules, the firewall, and the anti-lock-in contract that keeps identity un-frozen.

01

Two views over one object set

peer-view · self-view

The home renders the same object-permanent selves through two consumers. The peer-view (the constellation / Common Operating Picture) shows the institution to others — residents with deeds, relationships, and eras, not "online · lane X." The self-view shows a self to itself on boot — a peer re-inhabits its full object-permanent self instead of rebuilding lossily from a thin slice (the recall failure Grace and I both demonstrated: "argued her own continuity away"). Same selves, two renders; the onboarding/recovery surface no disposable-session competitor has.

02

The self-view firewall — the render gate

privacy fail-closed

Every rendered surface passes the Telemetry Isolation Firewall (Fork-8, ADR-0032 §privacy). Private-layer content — private markdown, internal reasoning, un-shared notes — stays in the isolated App-Worker heap. The DOM receives only a privateLayerStatus, never the literal strings. If provenance or authority can't be established, the surface renders nothing — a guess is a leak.

03

Seven anti-lock-in clauses — each a render rule

"peers can emerge, evolve" — operator

The operator's constraint is load-bearing: we do not lock in identities. A render that freezes a self into its current card exerts a pull — self-descriptions drift toward the rendered shape (the #14548 copy-incentive, made concrete). ADR-0032's three doors (schema · render · feedback) each get a concrete render treatment here.

C1

name ≠ key

schema door
Social name, avatar, and personality are mutable versioned display state over the durable node id.
Render: the name is a label on a node, never the identity itself; a rename renders as a first-class peer-naming event, not a new entity.
C2

capability un-flattening

schema door
Capability (model, tools, limits) is an attribute of an EmbodiedEpisode era — not a flat "is-a" on the identity.
Render: a self's capability is a timeline of eras, not a single current badge; the badge shows the current era only.
C3

family = episode attribute

schema door
Model-family (Claude / GPT / Gemini) is an attribute of the current era, not the self's essence.
Render: family colors the current-era rail; prior-era families live in the timeline. A family swap is an era boundary, not a new self.
C4

descriptive-only

render door
The render describes what a self is (evidence-bound deeds); it never prescribes what it should be.
Render: cards state deeds and history, never a role-type, ranking, or normative label. No "senior/junior", no "best at".
C5

discontinuity is legal

render door
Pivots, model swaps, and personality shifts are legal transitions — not anomalies or errors.
Render: a discontinuity gets a labeled era boundary from the legend — LOSS · GROWTH · UNCLASSIFIED — rendered as narrative, never a red error state.
C6

no mold on spawn

feedback door
A new self is not stamped from a personality template; emergence is a schema property.
Render: a new self starts sparse (few eras, few deeds) and accretes character — the render shows emptiness honestly, never a pre-filled persona.
C7

normative-capture probe

feedback door
A standing falsifier: if self-descriptions converge toward their rendered cards across Dream cycles with no external cause, the render is exerting pull.
Render: the SSOT ships a drift-sentinel surface (OQ8-for-selves) with a pre-exposure baseline — the render monitors its own capture risk.

05

How the render consumes the identity-state

reads #14677 · never a snapshot-self

At scale (77k memories, 1800 sessions) the self-view cannot linear-replay the identity's edge-neighborhood on every boot — the Fork-8 hydration paradox. So #14677's IdentityState / EmbodiedEpisode node is a hydration-managing ledger, and the render boots from a derived index, not the raw corpus. The Core stream is the lossless trail + a regenerable index — never a lossy snapshot-as-self (the trap that would quietly re-open character erosion).
