Two additive lines would exceed the 24 KiB per-file budget and duplicate the Claude surface, while a new skill would duplicate an existing lifecycle trigger. The denser line adds engine-primitive-first and base-family-suffix routing while reducing loaded bytes by 71. Retire each clause when a mechanical `apps/**` lint enforces it.

Decision Record impact: aligned-with ADR 0007.
