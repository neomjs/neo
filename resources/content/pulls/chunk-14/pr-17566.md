## Deltas from ticket

1. **The module→util-class conversion is extracted to its own follow-up leaf.** This PR moves the 15 modules to `apps/agentos/util/` unchanged (imports rewritten); wrapping them into `AgentOS.util.*` static classes rewrites every call site in 40+ files and belongs in a reviewable leaf of its own, not inside the 104-move mechanical pass. The `view/**` witness (law 1+3's guard) is complete either way; the util-class shape completes in the follow-up.
2. **ntypes stay unchanged.** They are registry keys, not base-class claims — renaming them would touch runtime consumers (`item.ntype ===` checks) for zero conformance gain. The ticket's "ntypes follow the new names" is dropped deliberately.
3. **`view/AccountsPanel.mjs` joined the pass** — the witness exposed `Accounts extends DashboardPanel` at the view root (the 29th offender, outside the fleet folder the ticket enumerated).
4. **Two dead files retired with receipts:** `SourceHealthMarker.mjs` + `fleetCardFactory.mjs` had zero importers repo-wide (only their own orphan specs referenced them); both + their specs + the orphaned SCSS deleted rather than moved.
5. The `cockpitDockDocument`-class modules (dock document, presets, start plan, lifecycle adapter, …) went to `util/` with the rest per law 3's letter; whether cockpit-scoped config modules should instead live as cockpit-local classes is a fair question for the util-class follow-up leaf.
