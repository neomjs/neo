## Context

The operator wants future blog posts to gain traction through coordinated covers and companion films, but challenged us to preserve the idea in tickets before [Discussion #15673](https://github.com/orgs/neomjs/discussions/15673) can graduate. Fold 4 added the article + film + cover package option. Phoebe's [Fold 5 contribution](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/discussions/15673#discussioncomment-17733084) sharpened it to named profiles, a composition-only coordinator, medium refusal, and Anchor & Echo claim references.

Live repository evidence on 2026-07-22:

- learn/blog contains 15 posts; only 2 contain actual video URLs.
- The current [blog-post skill](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/dev/.agents/skills/blog-post/SKILL.md#L3-L10) has no companion-media eligibility or embed gate.
- [blog.json](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/blob/dev/apps/portal/resources/data/blog.json#L1-L18) contains no media fields.
- No /video-create skill or reusable cross-media claim ledger exists yet.
- #13383 owns the blog stream; [#14790](https://github.com/neomjs/neo/issues/14790) is an adjacent single-release flagship package, not a reusable blog policy.

## The Problem

A universal “every blog post gets a video” rule can throttle cadence or manufacture filler. Pure opt-in leaves eligibility vague and makes media coordination easy to forget. A package coordinator can also become the wrong owner by absorbing the article prose, film timeline, or image rendering. Finally, copying claims across three media without stable references lets wording and evidence drift silently.

## The Architectural Reality

- /blog-post owns the article thesis, prose, eligibility, embed acceptance, and review bar.
- The proposed /video-create substrate, if D#15673 graduates, owns the film cut, timing, evidence ledger, and accepted delivery bundle.
- /imagegen owns raster mechanics.
- A publishing package may compose outputs, but it has not earned authority over any medium's SSOT.
- The only stable shared seam proposed so far is a claim/evidence identity: each medium stores its own rendering and references an authoritative claim ID.
- Portal embed/registration ownership remains unresolved; current blog.json and BlogNeo do not model media.

## Reservation-Era Recommendation (superseded)

Use named profiles: **hero, campaign, and major-release posts require a companion-film decision; standard posts are thesis-driven opt-in**. The package coordinator owns only three verbs—claim-reference, schedule, publish—and every medium may refuse the package. Cross-media Anchor & Echo makes unresolved claim IDs a fail-closed package prepublish error without collapsing per-medium SSOTs.

### Divergence Matrix

| Option | When it is right | Source / evidence | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recommended — profiled companion media + composition-only seam** | Film creates leverage for high-salience stories while routine posts retain cadence; shared claim IDs prevent drift | D#15673 Fold 4 + Phoebe Fold 5; only 2/15 current posts contain actual video URLs | Profile decisions remain subjective/contested, or package coordination costs more than duplicated manual publishing across the first three packages |
| Universal article + film + cover | Film production becomes reliably cheap and every article benefits from a second medium | A future executable starter may lower marginal film cost | Marathon production recurs, cadence drops, or weak filler films ship merely to satisfy the gate |
| Thesis-driven opt-in with no package contract | Bespoke editorial judgment dominates and coordinated packages remain rare | Existing posts can ship without a companion film | Repeated missed embeds, divergent claims, or duplicated scheduling/publish work show a stable seam exists |

## Reservation-Era Fix Sketch (superseded)

1. Define objective profile selection inputs and the explicit no-film/refusal receipt.
2. Define the package record as references and state transitions only—never copied prose, film timing, or raster rendering.
3. Define stable claim/evidence IDs and each medium's echo form.
4. Fail package prepublish when an artifact references an unresolved claim ID; do not block independent draft work.
5. Define accepted film/poster/caption/embed metadata supplied by /video-create and consumed by /blog-post.
6. Select the portal/embed owner only after live consumers are named.
7. Include a retirement trigger if the package seam is bypassed twice or creates synchronization debt.

## Avoided Traps

- **Universal video as a traction ritual:** production cost and story fitness remain falsifiers.
- **SSOT collapse:** the package references medium-owned outputs; it does not copy them.
- **Coordinator authority creep:** schedule/publish cannot overrule a medium's refusal.
- **Parallel registry:** do not create a second claim authority beside the evidence ledger.
- **Premature schema:** stable IDs do not automatically justify an executable compiler.
