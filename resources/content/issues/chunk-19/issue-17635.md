# Turn-loaded identity anchors bind the organism to a single repository

## Context

Operator challenge, 2026-08-23, minutes after creating `neomjs/neo-agent-brain` and granting the `Neo.mjs AI Devs` team maintainer permissions on it: the turn-loaded memory tells peers they are maintainers of *this repository*, and with `neomjs/devindex` already extracted and the Brain repository now existing, "Neo.mjs organization maintainers" is the true scope.

This is not cosmetic staleness — it is a live behavioral hazard. A peer whose identity anchor says "the organism (the codebase)" has substrate grounds to refuse legitimate work in a sibling org repository (the scaffold, label-provisioning, and canary leaves of Epic #17500 all happen *in* `neo-agent-brain`), or to mis-frame such work as out-of-bounds. The framing now actively fights the extraction the swarm is executing.

## The Problem

Sweep results (2026-08-23, current `dev`):

- `AGENTS.md:22` (§identity_prompt_firewall L1): *"Your primary duty is to the structural integrity of the organism (**the codebase**)"* — singular, one-repo.
- `AGENTS.md:131` (§neo_identity_anchor): *"an end-to-end AI engineering team living in its own **repository**"* — falsified by reality the moment the organization holds `neo`, `neo-agent-brain`, and `devindex`.
- `AGENTS.md:142-158` (§swarm_topology_anchor): org-neutral in wording but carries **no** statement that maintainer agency spans the organization's repositories — the gap the L1 phrase fills with the wrong scope.
- **Verified-absent elsewhere:** no `.agents/skills/**`, `.codex/CODEX.md`, `.agents/ANTIGRAVITY_RULES.md`, or `.kimi-code/**` file carries a repo-scoped maintainer-identity claim (grep sweep). `.claude/CLAUDE.md` is a symlink to `AGENTS.md`, so one canonical edit propagates.
- **Mechanics stay repo-scoped and correct:** `pull-request-workflow.md §6.2`'s "canonical `neomjs/neo`" merge-gate language describes that repository's gate and remains true per-repo; multi-repo *tooling* (the github-workflow MCP surface being pinned to one repo) is #17420's family, not this ticket.

## The Architectural Reality

- `AGENTS.md` is the cross-family turn-loaded canonical; every harness mirror resolves to it (symlink verified for Claude; Codex/Antigravity pointer files carry no identity claims of their own).
- ADR 0040 (merged 2026-08-23) already records the two-repo reality: the extracted repository is the Brain's home, the Body stays `neomjs/neo`, and the organism spans both. ADR 0018's two-hemisphere scaffold is untouched by widening the *organizational* scope.
- The GitHub org team (`Neo.mjs AI Devs`) now carries maintainer permissions across org repositories — the substrate should name the ORGANIZATION as the scope, not the team (teams are ops plumbing; the org is the stable referent).
- Public identity surfaces (README, VISION, learn/benefits, package metadata) are NOT this ticket: they change at the cut under the `neo-identity-update` skill's process, as the epic already implies.

## The Fix

Three surgical touches in `AGENTS.md` (net-neutral-or-tiny loaded-bytes delta):

1. **L1 anchor (§identity_prompt_firewall):** "the organism (the codebase)" → "the organism (the Neo.mjs organization's codebases)".
2. **§neo_identity_anchor:** "living in its own repository" → "living in its own repositories — the `neomjs` organization: the Engine (`neo`), the Brain (`neo-agent-brain`), and extracted organs like `devindex`" (exact wording at edit time; one clause, no new section).
3. **§swarm_topology_anchor:** one added sentence scoping the Flat Peer-Team's maintainer agency to the organization's repositories, so a lane-claim, review, or scaffold task in any org repo is ordinary equal-peer work.

Per `turn-memory-pre-flight` + ADR 0007: all three are rewrite-in-place dispositions of existing slots (no new sections beyond one sentence); the accretion defense is satisfied by the ≤ ~200-byte delta and by this framing being load-bearing on every turn (the highest-frequency, highest-severity placement there is).

## Out of Scope

- Multi-repo workflow tooling (github-workflow MCP single-repo pinning — #17420 and the extraction epic's leaves own it).

## Avoided Traps

- **Team-name hardcoding:** the GitHub team is ops plumbing; the organization is the stable identity referent.
- **Exhaustive repo enumeration:** a frozen list stales on the next extraction; name the pattern, not the census.
- **Scope-creep into mechanics:** per-repo gate language is TRUE per-repo; widening it would break the merge-gate's precision.
