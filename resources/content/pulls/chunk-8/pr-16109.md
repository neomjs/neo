## Summary

Renders the published live-lane-awareness projection at the Claude and Codex Stop-hook boundary without changing turn admission. The new reader is local, typed, independently bound, per-channel fresh, row/byte bounded, and fixed-order: lifecycle → global route → context-view invocation references.

Missing or invalid enrichment preserves the existing bare hook policy. Fresh legacy `lifecycle-state.json` data is no longer consulted by the active directive path.

## Deltas

- Adds `hookProjectionReader.mjs`, which validates the trusted target, categorical consumer binding, channel watermark/provenance, typed envelopes, and expiry before rendering.
- Adds config-owned projection path/binding leaves plus `maxRows` and `maxBytes`; the primitive has no density defaults.
- Integrates one shared reader into both Claude and Codex Stop hooks while keeping `admissionEffect: none`.
- Refuses foreign agent/instance projections, but preserves the independent global route for an explicit `route-only` binding.
- Renders stale/degraded/missing markers without rendering their rows and states truncation explicitly.
- Cuts the active Claude hook off the legacy lifecycle-state fallback while retaining the old utilities for the planned removal phase.
- Applies the issue-thread-authorized one-line ADR editorial clarification: binding ambiguity is a reader conclusion; missing/stale/degraded are channel states.
