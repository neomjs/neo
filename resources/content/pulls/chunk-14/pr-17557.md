Resolves #17554

Related: #17550

Neo now has a semantic list primitive for long fixed-height histories without importing Grid chrome or record-cardinality DOM. `Neo.list.Buffered` keeps the existing Store, ListModel, Navigator, `ul/li`, and component-row contracts while mounting only the viewport plus a symmetric row buffer between stable spacers.

Physical pool slots and logical records are separate truths. Surviving records retain their component slot where possible; list-item DOM stays in Store order; selection, focus, click mapping, sort/filter/load/recordChange, and prepend-heavy scroll anchoring all resolve through the logical record. A 5,000-record Store therefore owns nine components in the 5-visible + 2×2 fixture, not 5,000.

Evidence: L2 achieved (real Neo class + Store/Model/ListModel + VDOM-delta witnesses over 5,000 records, resize lifecycle, mutation, selection/focus, and anchor paths) → L2 required. Residual: none for this engine leaf; downstream consumer #17550 owns headed L3 scroll/product proof.

**Challenge (per guide §7.1):** `onScrollCapture` calls `createItems()` on EVERY captured scroll delta, not only at buffer-edge crossings. At the activity stream's scale this is invisible; at 60Hz scroll over a large pool it is measurable busywork. The repair is ALREADY half-shipped in this diff: you stamp `component.lastRecordVersion = record.version` (both branches of `getPooledComponent`) but never READ it — while your own cited precedent (`grid.column.Component:118`) reads exactly that field to SKIP reconfiguring a slot whose `record + version` are unchanged. Adopting the read side (skip `set()` when `component[recordProperty] === record && component.lastRecordVersion === record.version`) turns the per-scroll pass into near-no-ops and gives the stamp a purpose; today it is a write-only field a future reader will puzzle over. Non-blocking — fold it here if trivial, or let #17563 (recycling follow-up) own it; either way the stamp deserves one docblock sentence naming its intent.
