# Using the desktop

Every app opens in its own window. The apps you see are the ones your account may use.

## Windows

- **Open** an app from the desktop icons or the start menu.
- **Move** a window by its title bar, and **resize** it from any edge.
- **Minimize**, **maximize** or **close** it with the buttons at the right end of its title bar.
- The **taskbar** lists every open window. Click an entry to bring its window to the front.

## Apps

| App | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| Mail | Read the inbox. Select a message to show it in the reading pane. |
| Messages | Chat with the team. Press Enter to send. |
| Calendar | The team's week. Drag a meeting to move it. |
| Photos | The team's portraits, with their names. |
| Music | The library. Select a track to show it in the now-playing bar. |
| Documents | Shared files by folder. Delete moves a file to the Recycle Bin. |
| Recycle Bin | Deleted files. Restore puts a file back in Documents. |
| Settings | Your account, the theme and your permissions. |
| Reports | This week's usage per team. |

## Permissions

An account sees only the apps its permissions grant. Open **Permissions** in the taskbar, or the Permissions section
in Settings, to grant or revoke one on the live session: the desktop follows at once, and a window whose app you lose
closes.

## Signing out

**Sign out** in the taskbar closes every window and returns to the sign-in screen.
